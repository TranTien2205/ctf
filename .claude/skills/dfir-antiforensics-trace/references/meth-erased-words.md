# Method: The vocabulary that routes here

Depth module for `dfir-antiforensics-trace`. Open `../SKILL.md` for the
class contract first: first probe, falsifier, confirm shape, traps. This file
is one named piece of the corpus — never a finding by itself.

Question says: erased, cleared, wiped, hid, covered their tracks, deleted the logs — or the disguised form: 'what was the value of X' where X's artifact turns out empty. All of those are this class; the empty-artifact question is the hardest to route and the most common in bundles.

## Why

Anti-forensics answers are unusually easy to overclaim — every technique
here also has a benign producer (rotation, truncation, collection scope).
The two-answer rule and the falsifier block are what keep the class honest.

## Pivot

Method exhausted -> `rec-*` recovery columns; all columns dry -> the
falsifier block.
