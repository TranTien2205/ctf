Depth module for `dfir-antiforensics-trace`. Open `../SKILL.md` for the

Provider `Microsoft-Windows-Eventlog`, channel Security. The clear event is written INTO the fresh file, so its timestamp is the CLEAR time, never the intrusion time — the oldest trap in the class.
