# Reverse image search — name the place

Google Lens, Yandex, Bing visual — a hit names the place outright;
check more than one engine (Yandex often beats Google on places).
A similar-looking place is a lead, not an answer — confirm with an
independent feature.

## Lead not answer

A similar-looking hit is a lead — confirm by an independent feature (a plate, a sign, a pole) in the same frame.

## Multiple engines

Yandex often beats Google on places — check more than one engine before concluding a miss.
