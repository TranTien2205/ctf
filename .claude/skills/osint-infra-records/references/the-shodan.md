# Shodan/Censys — host fingerprints

Service banners + cert fingerprints for a host — what's running
and on what ports; a cert's other names leak siblings.

## What it gives

- Open ports + service banners.
- The cert's other SANs/hostnames.
- The host's OS/location/ISP.
A Shodan fingerprint corroborates a DNS-derived host.
