# Evidence shape: RSA key or plaintext recovery

Reference module for `ctf-writeup`. Writeup material per
bug-class family or writeup section: what a proving excerpt
looks like, what the section must state, what it must never
claim. The template stays in SKILL.md.

The proving excerpt a `RSA key or plaintext recovery` writeup needs:

## Claim -> evidence

The sentence the writeup makes, and the verbatim excerpt
that carries it - response bytes, decrypted output, or
artifact bytes depending on the class.

## Falsifier documented

The negative result that bounded the path, in the
four-field form.

## Common overclaim

The sentence this class's writeups most often overclaim,
and the evidence that would legitimately support it.
