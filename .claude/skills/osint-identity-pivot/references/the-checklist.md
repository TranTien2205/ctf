# The checklist

- [ ] The single most unique pivot chosen + normalised
- [ ] A plan of at most six queries, each with a named fact
- [ ] One source at a time, verbatim results
- [ ] Two-attribute corroboration before naming an identity
- [ ] Negatives recorded with their queries
- [ ] A too-generic pivot replaced, not forced

- [ ] Verbatim results, not paraphrase
- [ ] A second-order pivot (email→handle+domain) worked
