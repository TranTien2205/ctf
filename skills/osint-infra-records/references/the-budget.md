# The budget

TXT + a type sweep + whois + crt.sh + an AXFR attempt is cheap —
that covers the space; deeper enumeration only on a lead.

## The order of cost

- Cheap: one query per type, whois, crt.sh.
- Medium: AXFR attempt, a wordlist subdomain sweep.
- Expensive: a full brute-force + passive-DNS + archive dig.
Spend the cheap coverage first; the expensive passes on a lead.

## Spending it

TXT/sweep/whois/crtsh/AXFR is the cheap coverage — only a lead justifies the expensive passes (brute-force, deep passive).

## The close

Cheap coverage first; a lead justifies the deep pass; the falsifier closes the live space.
