TXT first, then the sweep, whois, cert+SANs. Confirm a sibling by
querying it. Quote the verbatim answer with the resolver + TTL.

turn (crt.sh/archive/passive).

## The whole method in three lines

1. TXT → sweep → whois → SANs → AXFR.
2. A found name is a lead until it resolves to a distinct answer.
3. The verbatim record + its source is the evidence.

## The discipline

No claim without the verbatim record; no count without the resolver; no sibling without a confirm.
