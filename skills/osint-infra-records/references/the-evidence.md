# Evidence — the verbatim answer

The verbatim record answer (a TXT string, a SAN hostname, a whois
field) + the source and date. A record that names a host is a
lead until that host is queried.

## Levels

- A record answer — a measurement; quote it verbatim.
- A hostname in a record — a lead until it resolves.
- A "where the flag lives" claim — needs the flag read, not just
  the record pointing at it.

## The provenance

Quote the resolver + the TTL + the source — a record answer is a
measurement of one moment; the provenance makes it re-checkable.

## The record IS the evidence

For an infra-records answer the verbatim record string is the finding — no further impact needed, but quote it exactly.

## Never

No claim of a flag from a record that only names a host — the flag must be read, not inferred.

## The standard

Verbatim + sourced + dated — a record answer is a measurement of one resolver at one moment.

## Lead vs answer

A record pointing at a host is a lead; the flag read from it is the answer — never conflate them.
