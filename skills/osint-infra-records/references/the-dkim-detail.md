# DKIM — selector._domainkey

`selector._domainkey.<d>` TXT records hold the mail-signing keys —
and a planted selector can carry a flag. Try common selectors.

## The query

`google._domainkey`, `mail._domainkey`, `default._domainkey`,
`s1._domainkey` — the selector varies; a found DKIM TXT holds a
key, and a planted one could hold a flag.
