# The chain card — recording what worked — checklist

Companion to `the-chain-card.md`.

## Checklist

- [ ] signals: the protection set + confirmed IP control.
- [ ] the offset and how it was measured.
- [ ] the gadget offsets (not absolute addrs under PIE).
- [ ] the leak source and the version identified.
- [ ] the chain shape and the ending.
- [ ] the traps: alignment, bad bytes, the libc build.
