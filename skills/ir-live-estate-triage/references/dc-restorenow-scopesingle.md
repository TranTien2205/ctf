# Decision condition: restore-now / scope-single

Routing module for `ir-live-estate-triage`. A live estate
already hit: services down, encrypted files, a note, a clock.
The router bounds the intake so evidence survives, then routes
to one dfir-* class.

How condition `scope-single` weights the `restore-now` decision.

## Read

Requires, favours, blocks, or irrelevant - the condition's
effect on this decision. A blocked decision states what
must happen first.
