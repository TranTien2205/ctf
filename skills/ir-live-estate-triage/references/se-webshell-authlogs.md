# Signal evidence: web-shell / auth-logs

Routing module for `ir-live-estate-triage`. A live estate
already hit: services down, encrypted files, a note, a clock.
The router bounds the intake so evidence survives, then routes
to one dfir-* class.

Whether `auth-logs` bears on the `web-shell` signal.

## Read

Primary, corroborating, or irrelevant - which captures
actually decide this signal. A signal without its primary
capture stays unverified.
