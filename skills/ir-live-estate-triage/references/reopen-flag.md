# When the flag is the answer

Routing module for `ir-live-estate-triage`. A live estate
already hit: services down, encrypted files, a note, a clock.
The router bounds the intake so evidence survives, then routes
to one dfir-* class.

An IR challenge asking 'the attacker's IP' or
'the implant path' - the captured evidence
answers it directly; no flag gate on a lab
answer but evidence still verbatim.
