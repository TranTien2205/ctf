# A count claim needs a denominator

"The IP hit 3000 times" is meaningless without the total and the
peers — the per-IP distribution plus wc -l gives it. Always pair
a count with the baseline.

## The shape of a count claim

- "IP X hit 3000 of 40000 total lines — 7.5%, vs the next IP at 200."
- "404s are 2% overall but 80% of IP X's requests."
The percentage and the peer comparison are what make it anomalous.
