## Read

What the feature contributes at this granularity - a country-level
tell narrows to a country family; a region-level tell narrows within;
an exact-level tell pins a candidate site.

## Confirm

This feature alone is a lead: the claim needs a second independent
feature agreeing at the same granularity. One feature, one
elimination, never a conclusion.
