## The monoalphabetic read

`σ(E)` is the top `ct` letter, `σ(T)` the next, ... — the `ct` frequency
*ranked* gives the `σ` skeleton: `σ(ct_top)≈E`, `σ(ct_2nd)≈T`, ... — the
anneal refines it.

## The polyalphabetic flatten

The `L`-coset mix flattens the `ct` freq toward uniform — no `ct` letter is
much commoner → the IC drops to ~0.038. Per-coset, the IC returns to ~0.066.

## The transposition preservation

`π` preserves the *multiset* — the `ct` has English letter counts but the
*order* is scrambled — the freq is English, the text is garbage.

## The digraphs/trigraphs

`TH`,`HE`,`IN`,`ER`,`AN` and `THE`,`AND`,`ING`,`ION` — the `σ` preserves them
as `σ(TH)` etc. — a `ct` trigraph frequency table anchors `σ`.

## The IC

`IC = Σ n_i(n_i-1)/N(N-1)` — English ~0.066, uniform ~0.038 — the mono/poly
split.
