# Onboarding — the first-session reads

## The one-liner

`cipher_id.py --text` → the family → `classical_solve <family> --text` →
`evidence.md` → `../field-notes.md`.

## The families

`caesar` (shift) · `vigenere` (polyalpha) · `subst` (bijection) · `railfence`
(permutation) · `atbash` (reflection) · `other-ciphers.md` (named others) ·
`enc_detect.py` (the encoding boundary).

## The signals

IC ~0.066/0.038 · chi² min `k` · Kasiski `L` · freq shape · charset.

## The procedure

`cipher_id` → `cipher-identification` → `classical_solve <family>` →
`evidence` → `field-notes`.

## The reads, in order

`../SKILL.md` → `detection.md` → `cipher-identification.md` → `workflow.md` →
`payload-dictionary.md` → `evidence.md` → the `<family>-deep.md` for your
family.
