# decompile order — dis first

dis.dis is the floor: always works on the matching version. uncompyle6/decompyle3/pycdc are conveniences for old versions; they can misdecompile obfuscated code.

## The route

Locate the check, read the transform, extract the expected, produce the input, verify on the real interpreter. See `the-workflow.md` and `evidence-depth.md`.
