Routing module for `rev-triage`. The pipeline is identify, decompile, find the deciding check, replicate it - this file is one named step or artifact; it never analyzes for you.

Whether `patch-check` solves the `xor-chain` check shape.

## Fit

The check's mechanism against the technique's capability -
a cmp-chain yields to strings-read; a transform yields to
inversion; a constraint yields to a solver. The cell states
the fit and the one-line reason.

## Cost

The technique's cost rank against the other cells in this
row - the cheapest fitting technique runs first.
